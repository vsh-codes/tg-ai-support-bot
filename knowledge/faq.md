# Frequently Asked Questions

> Replace this example file with your own FAQ content. The bot will
> index every `.md` and `.txt` file in this directory at startup.

## What is this service?

Replace this paragraph with a one-paragraph overview of what your company
does. Customers often start with this question, and a clear, concrete
answer here sets the tone for the rest of the conversation.

## How do I get started?

Describe the signup or onboarding flow. Include any links, codes, or
prerequisites a new customer needs to know about.

## What does it cost?

List your pricing tiers, free-trial details, billing cycle, and what's
included in each plan. If pricing depends on usage, explain the formula.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Walk through the cancellation steps explicitly. Customers asking about
cancellation are often anxious — clear instructions prevent escalation.

## How do I get a refund?

Describe your refund policy: what's eligible, what's the window, and how
the customer requests it.

## How do I update my payment method?

Step-by-step instructions for changing the payment method on file.

## Is my data secure?

Summarize your security practices: encryption at rest and in transit,
compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, etc.), and where data
is stored.

## How do I contact human support?

Describe how a customer can reach a human (email address, phone, hours).
The bot will offer this fallback when it can't answer.
